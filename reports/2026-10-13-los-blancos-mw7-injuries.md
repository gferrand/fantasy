### Cody Gakpo — HOLD

Ankle injury on 27 Sep 2026. He lasted 17 minutes of the Netherlands' 2-1 win in Serbia after a challenge from Sasa Lukic. Xavi said it was the ankle and hoped it was not serious ([Straits Times / Reuters, 28 Sep](https://www.straitstimes.com/sport/football/ankle-injury-forces-gakpo-out-of-dutch-squad); [ESPN, 28 Sep](https://www.espn.com.sg/soccer/story/_/id/50046932/cody-gakpo-injury-ankle-liverpool-netherlands-serbia)). He missed Manchester City on 11 Oct. Andoni Iraola said he was doing individual work and was not ready for that game. The same [Liverpool Echo piece on 12 Oct](https://www.liverpoolecho.co.uk/sport/football/football-news/isak-szoboszlai-gakpo-gravenberch-liverpool-34753246) reported that Dutch journalist Rik Elfrink had posted that Gakpo would be out for several weeks. On 13 Oct in Linz, Iraola said Gakpo, Szoboszlai, and Isak are "not far" but none had trained with the group, it is day to day, and he does not know if they will be ready for Brentford, Villarreal, or Brighton ([This Is Anfield, 13 Oct](https://www.thisisanfield.com/2026/10/szoboszlai-gakpo-isak-injury-latest-iraola-return-dates/)). [Premier Injuries](https://www.premierinjuries.com/teams/liverpool) rates him 25% for 17 Oct. The Elfrink line and the "not far" line disagree, so the multi-week read is the cautious one. MW7 at Brentford is unlikely. MW8 home to Brighton (25 Oct) is the first realistic game, MW9 home to Arsenal (1 Nov) more so. Season line before the injury: 0.64 xG, 1.71 xA, 371 minutes, 72.0 points. Core keeper. Hold on the bench.
