## How the points are built

Sleeper's matchup feed returns HTTP 404, so these are not Sleeper's stored `players_points`. Each projection is this league's live scoring weights applied to Opta counting stats. A check on five Los Blancos players: their season custom points equal the sum of the seven weekly custom points, and their Sleeper xG equals their FPL xG (Wissa 2.97, Calafiori 1.22, Leno 0, Schade 1.93, Barnes 1.24).

Goals and assists are not added on top of xG and xA. xG already includes penalties. ICT is shown in the source data and is not added again. A penalty-taker flag is used only as a note. In this league `pks` is a penalty save (it shows up on goalkeepers, +8), `pkd` is a penalty drawn (+3), and `pkm` is a penalty missed (−4).

Scoring weights used, per event, by position:

| Stat | D | M | F | GK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goal (from xG) | 9 | 9 | 9 | 10 |
| Assist (from xA) | 6 | 6 | 6 | 7 |
| Shot on target | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Key pass | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Accurate cross, chance created | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Aerial | 1 | 0.5 | 0.5 | 1 |
| Tackle won, interception, block | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Clearance | 0.25 | 0 | 0 | 0.25 |
| Clean sheet | 6 | 1 | 0 | 8 |
| Goal against | −1 | 0 | 0 | −1 |
| Save | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Dispossessed | −0.5 | −0.5 | −0.5 | −0.5 |
| Yellow / red | −1 / −7 | −1 / −7 | −1 / −7 | −1 / −7 |

Minutes score 0. Playing time matters only because it scales the rates and the chance of a clean sheet or a goal-against charge.

Rates are shrunk toward a normal player at that position. Reliability is minutes / 540, capped at 1. The prior is the median per-90 rate among players with at least 270 minutes (38 forwards, 85 midfielders, 94 defenders, 22 goalkeepers). Dasilva has 625 minutes, so his rate is not shrunk. Mateta is the exception in the next paragraphs: his attack rate is the healthy baseline, not the shrunk 91-minute sample.

Availability is week by week for players who are actually injured or doubtful. FPL "this round" is MW8. "Next round" is MW9. MW10 stays at 0 if next round is 0; a doubtful player keeps that percent; everyone else who is not injured goes to full availability. Injured, suspended, and unavailable stay at 0 even if the chance field says 100. Semenyo is that case. If the chosen chance is blank, a percent in the news text is used. Robertson's 75% comes from that text.

Two overrides sit on top of that, because the chance fields are not minutes:

- A drop or a start is checked against actual starts and minutes in the last two or three matches. Mateta's chance of 0 is dated 28 August and is ignored. Collins's 50/100 is ignored the same way. Kluivert, Cherif, and Damsgaard were checked on the element history, not the chance field.
- A player returning from a long injury is rated from his healthy baseline, then scaled by a labeled minutes ramp. Mateta is that case. The ramp replaces the chance field. It is not multiplied by it again.

Expected minutes already include availability, so the projection does not multiply by P(starts) a second time.

- If availability is under 100%, expected minutes are that percent times his average minutes in games he has already played.
- If he is fully available, expected minutes are 65% of his GW5–GW7 average, including zeros, plus 35% of his season minutes per gameweek, capped at 90.
- Mateta's 52 / 67 / 74 are the ramp above, not this formula. The formula on his 91 season minutes is what produced the withdrawn 1.74.

Clean sheets are credited only through the share of matches with 60 or more minutes. Goals against are charged in full for any appearance, including a short cameo. They are not scaled down by minutes. That matches how this league has paid those two stats.

Fixture math, split by component because a clean sheet and a goal are not the same kind of difficulty:

- Attack multiplier = opponent xGA per match / 1.491, times home 1.113 or away 0.887, clamped between 0.70 and 1.45.
- Defense multiplier = opponent xG per match / 1.508, times the opponent's venue factor, clamped between 0.75 and 1.40.
- Expected goals against λ = opponent xG × (team xGA / 1.491) × the opponent's venue attack factor, clamped between 0.20 and 3.80.
- P(clean sheet) = e^(−λ).

The 1.113 / 0.887 venue split is from goals, not xG: 113 home goals and 90 away goals in 70 finished matches. FPL's fixture file has no xG, and Understat's 2026 league file returns 404. The single "fixture multiplier" in the player tables is the component total divided by the same projection on a neutral fixture. It is a label, not the formula.

Projected points =

`attack per 90 × (minutes / 90) × attack multiplier`

`+ defense per 90 × (minutes / 90) × defense multiplier`

`+ flat per 90 × (minutes / 90)`

`+ P(60 minutes) × P(clean sheet) × clean-sheet weight`

`+ P(appears) × λ × goal-against weight`

Flat per 90 is cards, dispossessions, own goals, and penalty events.
